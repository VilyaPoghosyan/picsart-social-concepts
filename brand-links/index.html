How to read this

  • People are devices: one phone or computer counts once, even after several visits.
  • Each person is counted under the first tagged link they opened, and only what they did from that moment on is counted.
  • Recorded means Picsart’s own tracking saved the visit with our tag. Meta can count more people for the same ad (see Meta vs Picsart).
  • Meta click ID means the tap came from inside Instagram or Facebook. Without it the visit can still be real (Safari strips it, Pinterest never adds one), or it can be a link test or a link checker.
  • Ad 2 and Ad 3 carry the same ad ID in their links, so they share one row; Ad 3’s visits start Nov 12.
  • TikTok sent no tagged visits: TikTok bios have no link since Nov 9, and older TikTok links never showed up.
  • Nov 12 counts visits up to the time the data was pulled.
