What it means

  • 541K views turned into 23 real visits: 4.2 per 100K views.
  • Reelo has 81% of all views and 1 real visit.
  • Microdramas turns views into visits about 112x better than Reelo.
  • Microdramas got 13 new real visits on Nov 5, and AI tool users went from 4 to 13: its best day so far.
  • 214 more Microdramas visits on Nov 5 and 6 carry an ad id (utm_content 120250687970830069). No Picsart ad table knows it, so they are kept out of the totals. Only 3 used the AI tool.
  • 14 of 37 visits look like team link tests (no Meta click ID), the same 14 as on Nov 5.
  • The Reelo visit with a sign-in is an existing account, not a new user. 1 Microdramas visitor skipped sign-up on Oct 29.
  • Fix: tag every link with utm_source = platform, utm_medium = social_bio / social_dm / pin, utm_campaign = brand.
  • playground_social and the "microdrama" paid ads are other teams' campaigns, so they are not counted here.
