What it means

  • 719K views turned into 24 real visits: 3.3 per 100K views.
  • Reelo has 83% of all views and 1 real visit.
  • Microdramas turns views into visits about 141x better than Reelo.
  • 412 Microdramas visits from Nov 5 to 10 carry an ad id (utm_content 120250687970830069). No Picsart ad table knows it, so they stay out of the totals. Only 19 used the AI tool.
  • 46 more Microdramas visits on Nov 9 and 10 came through paid_social links (mostly ad id 120250743103570069). Paid microdrama campaigns are not ours, so they stay out too.
  • 14 of 38 visits look like team link tests (no Meta click ID), the same 14 as on Nov 6.
  • The Reelo visit with a sign-in is an existing account, not a new user. 1 paid visitor also has an account. 1 Microdramas visitor skipped sign-up on Oct 29.
  • First tagged Pinterest visit (Dwell, Nov 7). Pinterest gives no click ID, so it cannot be told from a test.
  • Fix: tag every link with utm_source = platform, utm_medium = social_bio / social_dm / pin, utm_campaign = brand.
  • playground_social and the "microdrama" paid ads are other teams' campaigns, so they are not counted here.
