What we are doing

Five social media brands run end to end on Picsart GenAI. An automated pipeline writes, generates, quality-checks and publishes about ten new posts a day on Instagram and TikTok plus five Pinterest pins; one person reviews and approves, no production team. Every post carries "made with Picsart". Running since Oct 3.

The goal

Millions of engagements, converted into Picsart users: every post is labelled "made with Picsart", and keyword posts send viewers the exact Picsart steps by DM. Five concepts run side by side to learn which formats earn reach, which earn engagement and what each costs on Picsart's premium models (Seedance 2.5, Gemini 3 Pro Image, Nano Banana Pro).
