Social automation at Picsart

Status as of 2026-10-26.

What we are doing

Five social media brands run entirely on Picsart GenAI: an automated pipeline writes, generates, quality-checks and publishes four to eight posts a day on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest, every one labelled "made with Picsart". One person reviews and approves; no production team. Running since Oct 3.

Why

To grow social channels to millions of engagements without manual work, and to prove at scale what Picsart's premium models (Seedance 2.5, Gemini 3 Pro Image, Nano Banana Pro) can produce: five concepts tested side by side show which formats earn reach, which earn engagement, and what each costs.

The goal

Grow these channels to millions of engagements and convert that audience into Picsart users: every post is labelled "made with Picsart", and keyword posts send viewers the exact Picsart steps by DM. The system should run itself, with human work under 30 minutes a day. Each concept gets a scale-or-stop decision after 12 posts, on fixed dates (Nov 21, Nov 28, Dec 5).
