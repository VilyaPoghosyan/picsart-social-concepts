If you read only this

What
A faceless page (no presenter on camera) about making money with AI content. Each post shows something that can earn, how it was made in Picsart, and a Receipt: real cost, tools, the rule that decides if it pays.
Who
People who want to earn from content without showing their face. Earning means posting every week, so they need the tools every week.
Why Picsart
A faceless video usually needs four paid apps; Picsart does every step in one. Each post hands the viewer the exact prompts to run there.
How
Every Monday an AI agent picks the week's viral AI formats; Picsart's AI makes the assets; software cuts the posts; a person approves. About 20 to 60 credits (Picsart's unit of payment for AI) per video.
Will it work
Main metric: median views against our new-account baseline. Picsart metric: guide requests per 1,000 views; decision after 12 posts over 4 weeks.
Evidence
Strong on demand and the gap: in our scan, no honest "make money with AI" page above about 150K followers. Weak on money from views alone, so every post shows the real payout path, never a promise.

The five post types, with the real examples we made

A pillar is a recurring type of post. A Reel is a short vertical video, a carousel a swipeable set of images, the hook the line on screen in the first second. All five examples were made with Picsart's AI; every caption ends "made with Picsart".

1 · Build It, Reel, 35 s. The how: the finished episode first, then each step with its real output, then the Receipt. "AI monkey vlog, start to finish", 64 credits. Ends "Comment BUILD and I'll send you the template".
2 · Works Right Now, Reel, 30 s. The reach: this week's viral AI format, remade with our own characters. A houseplant soap opera, episode 1. Ends "Follow, tutorial tomorrow": the episode and its tutorial form a thread.
3 · Earn Drop, Reel, 22 s. The first dollar: Earn with Picsart pays creators for posts made with Picsart, with no follower minimum. We show a live brief, an example entry and how to enter. Runs only when a brief is live; this one is modelled on a past brief.
4 · Does It Pay?, carousel, 7 slides. The trust: real numbers, the rules, a green, amber or red verdict, a source on every slide.
5 · One App, carousel, 6 slides. The switch: the eight jobs behind one faceless video, all in one Picsart wallet, with our real credit total.

How it is made

Per post

Reel: about 20 to 60 credits (estimate). Carousel: 0 to 5.

Per week

7 posts on Instagram and TikTok: 5 Reels (Build It, Works Right Now, Earn Drop when a brief is live) and 2 carousels (Does It Pay?, One App). About 150 to 250 credits, 10 human minutes.

Rules on every post

AI label on · "made with Picsart" · a source on every number · never "you will make $X" · no real people, celebrities or kids.

Will it work, and how we'll know

Hypothesis. Would-be faceless creators will follow a page that shows real results with real costs, and many will ask for the guide and run it in Picsart.

MetricTarget to scale
Median views per post (main)above 3× our own new-account baseline
Guide requests + link taps per 1,000 views (Picsart usage)rising week over week
Sends per reach (Instagram's top signal) and follows per 1,000 viewsrising; positive follower delta

First experiment. "Comment the keyword for the guide" against "Follow, tutorial tomorrow": six posts each, measured in guide requests plus follows per 1,000 views.

Scale after at least 12 posts over 4 weeks if median views beat 3× the baseline and followers grow: move toward two posts a day.
Kill after 12 posts if median views stay under 1.5× the baseline and no post hit 10× the page's median: replace it from the backlog within a week.

Honest read: demand and the gap are well evidenced; the risk is trust. It only works if every claim is sourced and some verdicts say "this does not pay".
