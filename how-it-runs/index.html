Picsart Social · system walkthrough · 6 Nov 2026

How our brands run themselves

Five brands post every day with no production team. Scheduled AI agents decide what to make, generate it with Picsart models, check it with measured tests, schedule it, and report back. A person only vetoes, answers alerts and sets direction. This page shows every agent, every model and every step.

5brands: Reelo, Dwell, Listed, Faceless, Microdramas
~10videos and carousels a day, plus 5 Pinterest pins
17agents on a schedule; 7 more kept as manual backups
3in-house tools every agent must use: ops, qa, drama
13measured quality checks before anything can be scheduled
1,500credit floor: below it nothing new is made
1 · The big picture

Every post goes through the same six stages

Each brand has its own creative recipe (a "skill") and its own scheduled agent. Everything that can be a rule is code; the AI is used only for creative and judgment work. No stage can be skipped: the scheduler refuses a post that has no passing quality record.

STAGE 1

Decide

Check how many days of posts are left. Pick ideas from the brand's idea bank, its cast and what performed best.

runway · scorecard · idea banks
STAGE 2

Write

Story sheet, brief or script for each post, checked by a validator or a script critic.

brand skill · Claude
STAGE 3

Make

Picsart models make the stills, video, voices and music. Free local tools assemble, caption and add the end card.

Picsart MCP · ffmpeg · compositor
STAGE 4

Check

Measured tests (faces, text, end card, voice, subtitles…) plus an AI critic. A failure gets one retake, then the post is dropped.

qa gate · qa-critic
STAGE 5

Schedule

Preview, then publish to Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest at fixed slots, with AI labels and "made with Picsart".

ops schedule → Zernio
STAGE 6

Measure

Daily and weekly reports. A scorecard ranks every post 48 h after it goes live.

reporting agents · ops scorecard
↺ Feedback loop The scorecard is read by the next day's planners: the formats, characters and story patterns with the best median views get more slots.

Where it runs

Scheduled tasks in the Claude desktop app on one Mac. Each run is a Claude agent with the brand's instructions, the Picsart connector and the three in-house tools.

Who publishes

Zernio, a social publishing service, holds the connected Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest accounts. Agents never post directly on the platforms.

What the human does

Vetoes posts from a daily 48-hour sheet, answers problem alerts, tops up credits, approves direction changes. No routine approvals.

3 · Brand by brand

Six pipelines, one shape

Every pipeline starts the same way (open a run record, read the credit balance, check the queue, retry any failed post once) and ends the same way (quality gate, schedule, log, close the run). What changes is where ideas come from, which models make the media, and which checks apply.

Reelo · Instagram + TikTok

15-second Pixar-style family cartoons

A toddler, a parent and a pet in a tiny story told with faces and sound, no dialogue, ending on the Picsart end card.

How a reel is made

  1. Write six-beat story sheets from the story engine (12 patterns), the premise bank and the locked cast of 11; no premise repeats within 60 days.
  2. A validator checks every sheet (up to 2 rewrite rounds).
  3. Make the keyframe with the cast's reference images; one reroll if the look is off.
  4. Make the 15 s reel with native sound effects in one Seedance take.
  5. Finish locally: 1080×1920, 30 fps, loudness −14.5 LUFS, end card.
  6. Quality gate + critic; a failed reel is held and the next sheet takes its slot.
  7. Schedule both platforms, mark the premise used, log.

Ideas come from

  • Story engine + premise bank in the skill ("invent first, bank second")
  • 26 analysed reference reels (30K–9.7M likes)
  • Scorecard: the best-median story pattern gets a slot every day (now P11)

How a skit is made

  1. Plan with the shuffle rules: 10 characters × 6 specialists, no product or roast repeated in the last 20.
  2. Make the product "before" and two start frames, checked on one sheet.
  3. Make the real "after" with actual Picsart tools only (from a verified tools list).
  4. Two voiced Seedance takes; a local transcript must match the script, or retake.
  5. Build: captions, option cards, logo, sound, end card.
  6. Quality gate + critic + eye checks; schedule with the "comment FIX" auto-DM.

How a week is made

  1. Pick 7 source videos from our other brands (live 2+ days, unused, with logged credits): 3 cartoon, 2 drama, 2 interior, shifted by the scorecard.
  2. Prices only from a checked price bank, with at least one new sourced price a week.

How an episode is made

  1. The engine says what's next: make, approve a series, or alert.
  2. Write the episode; a script linter and a script critic must pass (2 rewrites max).
  3. Make start frames; check every face against the cast and every gaze direction.
  4. Make voiced Seedance takes using each character's locked voice sample.
  5. Transcribe every take, check faces again; a critic picks the best take.
  6. Cut, fix blinks at cuts, build the master with subtitles.
  7. All 11 checks + a gating episode critic (min 7 / mean 7.3), then schedule.

Ideas come from

  • Friday research: top charts of ReelShort, DramaBox, ShortMax and leading AI-drama accounts (last 60 days)
  • Scored premise candidates (hook, mystery, conflict, appeal, novelty)
  • Series bible: cast, 8-part arc, mystery plan

6 · The engine

Three in-house tools do every non-creative step

Agents are not allowed to improvise shell commands; they call these three tools. This keeps runs predictable, logged and safe to repeat.

ops

Operations: runway, scheduling, credits, logs, alerts, scorecard.

status
Days queued, failed posts, account health, credits per brand.
schedule … --go
Uploads, creates one post per platform, verifies it, writes the ledger. Preview by default.
retry
Retries each failed post once.
credits record / budget
Logs the balance; compares today's spend to the brand's cap and the floor.
run start / end · watchdog
Records every run; finds stuck or missed ones.
alert
Records an alert and says whether to send it (same alert max once per 24 h).
upcoming · pull
The 48 h veto sheet; removes a vetoed post.
scorecard
Ranks posts 48 h+ old; writes a line for the planners.
qa

Quality: measured checks, all local, 0 credits. Writes qa.json next to each post.

probe · letterbox
Size, fps, length; no black or white bands over 2%.
motion · text · endcard
No frozen opening; no stray text (OCR); Picsart end card present.
faces · strangers
Face recognition: same person in every shot (≥ 0.45), no strangers.
eyeline · blinks
Who looks where; no eyes closed at a cut (auto-fix).
voice · transcript · subs
Each actor's own voice; every line heard word for word; every word subtitled.
cast-score
Ranks casting candidates for consistency, novelty and appeal.
critic
The qa-critic scores the brand rubric. Blocks posts on Microdramas; advisory elsewhere until calibrated.
drama

Microdrama engine: a state machine for each episode of each series.

next
planned → scripted → linted → producing → built → gated → scheduled.
lint
14–19 shots, 45–60 s, 10–12 short lines, premise in 5 s, follows the mystery plan.
refusal ladder
If the video model refuses a shot: rephrase, rephrase, restage, voice-over, then alert. Never drops a line.
credits · blocked
Stops a part at 2,900 credits or after 2 holds and asks the operator.
propose · approve · veto
Friday series pick; starts only after the Monday veto window.
build
Assembles the cut, subtitles and a 1440×2560 master.

7 · Scheduling and publishing

How a post gets out, and why it never double-posts

Before anything is scheduled

  • The media file exists and matches the brand's size, frame rate and length.
  • The caption contains "made with Picsart" and stays within the hashtag limit.
  • A passing qa.json exists for this exact file. If the file changed after the check, it is refused as stale.
  • Publishing is unlocked by two switches set by the operator; without them every publish command is blocked.
  • Instagram posts carry the AI-generated flag; TikTok posts carry "made with AI" and the required consents.

While scheduling

  • Every command previews first; only --go sends.
  • One upload per post, one post per platform, at the brand's fixed slots.
  • Each post is read back to confirm it is really scheduled.
  • A ledger records every post; an already-scheduled or removed post is never sent again, and each send carries a fixed request key.
  • A failed post is retried once the next morning; a second failure alerts the operator.
  • A vetoed post is deleted and marked "removed" so nothing brings it back.
8 · Reports and learning

How numbers flow back into what gets made

The scorecard

Every post 48 hours or older gets its views, likes, comments, shares and saves, joined with what the post was (format, character, story pattern). Missing numbers count as "no number", never as zero. It writes one line per brand that the planners read.

  • Reelo: the best-median story pattern gets a slot every day; the worst at most once a week.
  • Dwell: Monday's planner moves up to 2 posts from the weakest format to the strongest.
  • Listed: characters well below the channel median sit a batch out.
  • Faceless: the best source type gets one more slot a week.
  • Microdramas: each script reads what held viewers; new series come from outside research.

The reports

  • Daily, per brand: runway, failures, account health, credits, top posts. Written to the workspace; sent to nobody.
  • Friday, per brand: a scorecard with the scale/kill rule (after 12 posts: scale at 3× the baseline, kill under 1.5×). It recommends; it never decides.
  • Friday, team pages: the all-brands report and executive summary, plus a password-protected weekly page.
  • The credit ledger attributes every drop in balance to a brand, so reports show all-in cost per post.
9 · Safety rails and the human role

What the agents can never do, and what still needs a person

Hard rules, enforced by code

  • Credential files can't be opened by any agent.
  • Nothing publishes unless the operator's publish switches are on.
  • Slack: agents can only DM the operator. No channel posts, no messages to anyone else.
  • Every post carries the AI label, "made with Picsart" and the Picsart end card.
  • Each brand has a credit cap; nobody spends below the 1,500 floor.
  • Faceless prices must come from a checked price bank, and we only show Picsart tools that exist in the product.

What the operator does

  • Daily, ~5 min: look at the 48 h sheet; reply "pull …" for anything wrong.
  • On alert: runway low, a failed post, a stuck run, a blocked drama part, or credits near the floor (ask the credit team for a top-up).
  • Friday: optional veto of the next drama series; read the weekly reports.
  • Direction: new brands, formats, cadences, budgets and brand rules.
10 · Known gaps

What isn't solved yet

biggest

It runs on one laptop

Tasks only run while the Mac is on and the Claude app is open. Missed runs catch up later (nothing ran on 31 Oct–1 Nov). A run that hits a permission prompt waits until someone approves it. Needs an always-on runner and service accounts.

data

Some numbers don't arrive

Pinterest shows 0 views in our feed, Faceless has no analytics, and Microdramas TikTok numbers stopped updating on 30 Oct. Instagram lags by hours. Public-page counts fill some gaps.

quality

The critic only blocks on dramas

On Reelo, Dwell and Listed the AI critic is advisory until it is calibrated against labelled posts. There is no automatic loudness check yet; Pinterest is checked by eye.

process

The 48 h veto isn't enforced

The scheduler can book a post 15 minutes ahead, and posts booked after 09:30 skip that day's sheet.

cost

Credit attribution overlaps

When two agents run at once, the whole balance drop goes to one brand; 2,966 credits on 4 Nov were never attributed.

learning

No formal experiments yet

Planners learn from the scorecard, but the experiments log has no pre-registered tests besides Faceless's call-to-action test. Listed and Faceless are on Instagram only, with TikTok being added.
