Dwell

@dwell.rooms · @dwellrooms · Instagram + TikTok + Pinterest

Interiors, decoded. Empty rooms turned into modern, old-money and chic luxury interiors for designers and people collecting ideas. Every room is made with Picsart AI.

Key concept
Format15–23 s reels and 7–8 slide carousels on Instagram and TikTok; 2:3 room photos on Pinterest.
LookMarble, plaster, lacquer, travertine and brass, warm-neutral light, one palette per post, small spaced Gilroy capitals.
EngineRooms on Gemini 3 Pro Image, camera moves on MiniMax and Kling V3, beat-synced ElevenLabs music, the linen and walnut Picsart end card.

Live on 3 of Dwell's 4 platforms; the X account is not posting and is left out.
