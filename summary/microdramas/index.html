Picsart Social · brand summary

Microdramas

@microdramas.ai · Instagram + TikTok

Serialized AI love stories shot like a 1990s Hollywood film: 26–57 second episodes with spoken dialogue and cliffhanger endings, new parts most days. Every frame, voice and cut is made with Picsart AI.

Key concept
FormatVertical 9:16 episodes of 8-part series. Each part ends on a question, so viewers come back for the next one.
Look35 mm film, candlelight, satin and tuxedos, rainy streets. A recurring cast with fixed faces and voices per series.
EngineKeyframes on Nano Banana Pro and Seedream, continuous takes with native dialogue on Seedance 2.5, finished with the Picsart end card.

Series so far: SPEAK NOW · THE LAST BITE · FAMILY PORTRAIT · REMEMBER ME?. 15 episodes live, one season (SPEAK NOW) complete.
