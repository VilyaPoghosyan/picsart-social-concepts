Picsart Social · brand summary

Reelo

@reelo.hq · @reelostudio · Instagram + TikTok

Short AI videos that stop the scroll. Live since 2026-07-31: first AI-trend explainers and miniature clips, then from September "tiny worlds", a ruined miniature place restored in one video a day. From Oct 17 the channel switches to 15-second Pixar-style family cartoons, four a day.

Key concept
Live now · tiny worlds25–28 s restorations of a ruined miniature (a fire station, a cable car) with a voiceover and captions. One a day.
From Oct 17 · cartoons15 s micro-stories about a toddler, a parent and a pet, told with faces and sound effects, no dialogue. A cast of 11, shuffled.
EngineTiny worlds: Gemini 3 Pro Image stills, Veo 3.1 clips, ElevenLabs voice. Cartoons: Gemini 3 Pro Image keyframe, Seedance 2.5 with native sound.

Cartoons go out at 12:00, 13:30, 18:00 and 19:30 Yerevan; the first four are scheduled for Oct 17 and cost 110–120 credits each, against 416–436 for a tiny-world video. Reelo's Pinterest and X accounts are not posting and are left out.
