Meta counts every tap; Picsart only records the people whose page loaded, so the gap is people who closed Instagram's browser first. That is about 10 cents for each person who reached Picsart. One ad is still running, until Nov 16.

What the data says

  1. The ads bring the most people, and most leave at once. 728 came from Microdramas ads; 596 left within 10 seconds, 3 pressed Generate, 1 created an account, nobody got a result.
  2. Rich Ronnie brings fewer, warmer visitors. 50 people, median 12 seconds on the page; 15 pressed Generate, 2 created an account and 1 got a video, the only result from an Instagram visitor so far.
  3. The sign-in wall stops almost everyone. 20 of the 21 people who pressed Generate were blocked because they had no account.
  4. Nobody has paid yet. No purchase from any of our links since Oct 15.
  5. Story link taps are still unread. Instagram shows them only inside the app, so Rich Ronnie's and Granny Ofik's story taps are pending.
