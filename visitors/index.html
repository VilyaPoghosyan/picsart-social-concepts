Why nobody could make anything

  • On phones, the cookie banner covers that button until it is closedyes

  • Our old link: Seedance 2.5 video, 15 s, 1080p270 credits

What the data says

  1. The ad brings people, but they leave in 2 seconds. 88% of ad visitors leave within 10 seconds, almost all inside the Instagram app.
  2. Nobody generated anything. Our link opens a 15-second 1080p Seedance video setup, which needs an account and credits. No visitor reached a result.
  3. The wall comes before the result. The only person who pressed Generate was blocked, saw sign-up, and left.
  4. Bio-link visitors are warmer. They stay 23 seconds instead of 2, and 10 of 20 typed a prompt.
  5. The video was out of reach even after sign-up. A new account gets 5 credits; our link opened a 270-credit video. The second and third ads land on a 5-credit video instead.
  6. First sign-up, still no result. One visitor of the second ad signed up on Nov 11. Nobody has generated a video from any brand link yet.
