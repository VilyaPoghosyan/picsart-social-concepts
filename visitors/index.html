What the data says

  1. The ads bring the most people, and most leave at once. 785 came from the Instagram app through Microdramas ads; 638 left within 10 seconds, 4 pressed Generate, 1 created an account, nobody got a result.
  2. Rich Ronnie's account brings the warmest visitors. 104 from the Instagram app, mostly India and Android; 27 pressed Generate, 3 created an account and 1 got a video, the only result from an Instagram visitor so far.
  3. The sign-in wall stops everyone at first. All 32 people who pressed Generate were stopped because they had no account.
  4. Nobody has paid yet. No purchase from any of our links since Oct 15.
  5. Story link taps are still unread. Instagram shows them only inside the app, so they are pending.
